About Me

I am a first-year student in the BBA in Digital Buisness and Entrepreneurship programme at IIM Bangalore. While I immerse myself in the world of business and strategy, my heart beats for filmmaking.

I am driven by a deep curiosity — the kind that makes me pause at golden-hour light, notice the rhythm of a conversation, or wonder how a single frame can hold an entire emotion. Creativity, for me, is not a skill; it is a way of seeing.

I believe the best stories live somewhere between structure and instinct, between business logic and pure artistic instinct. This is the space I am exploring and Want to lunch a startup combining with filmmaking skills and Digital Entrepreneurship.
